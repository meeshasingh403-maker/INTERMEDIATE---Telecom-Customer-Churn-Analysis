# Intermediator Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

A telecom company has experienced a steady decline in its customer base over the past year. The objective is to understand why customers are leaving, identify high-risk customer segments, and explore opportunities to improve customer retention.

The key business questions addressed in this project are:

- **Why are customers leaving?** Identify patterns and factors associated with customer churn.
- **Which customer groups have the highest churn?** Compare churn across different customer segments.
- **What factors are related to churn?** Explore the relationship between billing, pricing, contract types, service quality, and usage patterns.
- **How much revenue is being lost?** Assess the revenue associated with churned customers to understand the potential business impact.
- **Which retention actions should be prioritized?** Identify customer segments and business areas that may benefit from targeted retention strategies.

## 2. Dashboard Goal

The goal of this dashboard is to transform raw telecom customer data into meaningful business insights using Power BI and Power Query.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of customer churn, customer segments, and relevant business metrics. It helps stakeholders explore churn patterns, understand potential contributing factors, assess revenue at risk, and identify areas where customer retention efforts could be focused.

Rather than relying on assumptions, the dashboard supports data-driven investigation and helps the business make more informed decisions about customer retention.

## 3. Key Dashboard Visuals

- **Clustered Bar Chart:** Compare customer churn across different customer categories and segments.
- **Bar Chart:** Analyze churn patterns across relevant service, contract, or billing categories.
- **Column Chart:** Compare customer-related metrics across selected categories to identify differences and trends.
- **Bar Chart:** Examine another key churn dimension to highlight customer groups that may require attention.
- **Table:** Present detailed customer or category-level information for closer analysis and comparison.
- **KPI Cards:** Display important summary metrics, such as total customers, churned customers, churn rate, and relevant revenue metrics, where available.
- **Interactive Slicers and Filters:** Allow users to explore the data by selected customer attributes and categories.

## 4. Key Insights

The dashboard is designed to help explore:

- Customer segments with comparatively higher churn.
- Differences in churn across contract, billing, and service categories.
- Customer characteristics and usage patterns associated with churn.
- The financial significance of customers who have left the company.
- Areas where targeted retention strategies may be worth investigating.

Actual findings should be based on the results displayed in the dashboard rather than assumed in advance.

## 5. Business Impact

The analysis can support telecom business teams in several ways:

- **Improved Customer Retention:** Identify customer segments that may need additional attention.
- **Revenue Protection:** Understand the financial impact associated with customer losses.
- **Better Decision-Making:** Use data to investigate churn patterns instead of relying only on assumptions.
- **Targeted Retention Strategies:** Help prioritize retention efforts based on observed customer behavior.
- **Operational Insights:** Highlight billing, contract, and service categories that may warrant further investigation.

## 6. Tools and Technologies

- Power BI — Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- Power Query — Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
- Kaggle — Dataset source

## 7. Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how business intelligence tools can be used to investigate telecom customer churn, analyze customer behavior, and communicate business insights through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard provides a foundation for understanding customer attrition, evaluating potential revenue implications, and supporting more informed customer retention decisions.
